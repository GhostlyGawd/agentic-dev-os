# Project Brief

## Problem
AI-assisted software teams gain speed but lose product intent, scope discipline, traceability, and trustworthy operational evidence as work scales.

## Users
AI-native engineering teams, platform teams, developer-tool teams, and teams with governance or audit needs.

## Desired Outcome
Teams can move from evidenced customer problems to verified delivery without relying on chat memory or losing the reason work exists.

## Business Value
Lower rework, safer automation, faster review, clearer accountability, and auditable delivery evidence.

## Constraints
- Time: workflows must remain lightweight enough for daily use.
- Budget: the core uses no paid runtime dependency.
- Team: artifacts must work for small teams and scale to multiple owners.
- Technical: Python 3.11 standard library and repository-native files.
- Legal/compliance: no credentials or production personal data in events.

## Out of Scope
- Autonomous production operation.
- Vendor-specific publishing without explicit credentials and authorization.
- Replacing human product judgment.

## Definition of Done
- Product intent traces through implementation and outcomes.
- Risk and scope controls fail closed.
- Local and remote CI evidence is reproducible.

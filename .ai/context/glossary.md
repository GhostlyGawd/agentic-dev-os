# Glossary

- Outcome (`O-NNN`): measurable user or business change.
- Opportunity (`OP-NNN`): evidenced user problem contributing to an outcome.
- Experiment (`EXP-NNN`): smallest test of an assumption or opportunity.
- Bet (`BET-NNN`): bounded hypothesis with success, kill, and advance criteria.
- Milestone (`M-NNN`): reviewable increment tied to a bet and PRDs.
- Change request (`CR-NNN`): governed proposal to alter approved scope.
- Review (`REVIEW-NNN`): human milestone or release decision.
- PRD requirement (`PRD-NNN-RNN`): canonical delivery requirement.
- Proofstack: the niche-discovery product (repository `niche-finder`) that adopted this operating system; its product chain lives in that repository under its own `.ai/` layer.
- Dogfood issue: a product gap discovered through real usage of our own products, recorded with evidence before any delivery commitment.
- Synthetic demo hypothesis: seeded example data (fictional evidence and access claims) that demonstrates a product flow; it must be labeled synthetic at creation and never counts as market validation (FINDING-001).

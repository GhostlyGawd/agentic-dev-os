# BET-002: Proofstack Pre-Hypothesis Exploration Funnel

## Linked Outcome
O-002

## Linked Opportunity
OP-002

## Hypothesis
We believe a first-class guided pre-hypothesis funnel in Proofstack — capture raw market signals and sources, explore several markets and recurring workflows, drill into candidate segments (roles, consequences, alternatives, accessibility), compare candidates without pretending they are validated, and promote one candidate into a falsifiable investigation with provenance attached — will let founders discover which hypotheses deserve investigation, because today the product only manages and validates hypotheses it is handed.

## Smallest Valuable Version
A single-user funnel covering the five stages: signals and sources as first-class records; market and workflow exploration notes; candidate segment drill-downs; a side-by-side candidate comparison that carries an explicit unvalidated state; and a promote action that creates an investigation pre-linked to its signals, candidates, and comparison rationale. Existing investigation, validation, experiment, and productize flows stay unchanged downstream of promotion.

## Success Signal
EXP-002 passes its threshold: one real dogfood exploration reaches a promoted, falsifiable investigation with full provenance and zero synthetic demo records used as evidence.

## Kill Criteria
Dogfood sessions show we reliably arrive with an already-formed hypothesis (A-004 false), the funnel cannot represent candidates without implying validation, or promotion-time provenance is routinely ignored downstream (A-005 false).

## Advance Criteria
EXP-002 is recorded with its success threshold met and A-004 through A-006 resolved. Only then draft the Proofstack funnel PRD and milestone in this repository and open implementation tickets against the `niche-finder` repository. A proposed bet cannot advance without this evidence.

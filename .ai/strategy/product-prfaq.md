# PR/FAQ

## Press Release

The following release narrative works backward from the intended customer outcome.

### Headline
Agentic Development OS connects customer outcomes to verified software delivery.

### Summary
Teams can now govern strategy, discovery, bets, implementation, and operational evidence in one repository-native chain.

### Customer Problem
AI agents can implement well-specified work while the team still chooses the wrong work or loses the reason it matters.

### Solution
A validated upstream product layer feeds approved bets into the existing PRD, spec, ticket, trace, test, and metric system.

### Benefit
Teams gain speed without turning product direction into untraceable feature accumulation.

## Internal FAQ

These questions define the investment rationale, timing, success, and failure conditions.

### Why This?
The delivery system already governs execution; this closes the strategy-to-PRD gap.

### Why Now?
The master system is complete and can enforce the added relationships without creating a parallel stack.

### What Will Success Look Like?
Every active PRD links to a validated bet and every shipped requirement links to an outcome.

### What Could Make This Fail?
Duplicated sources, ceremonial artifacts, unevidenced opportunities, and metrics without product review.

# North Star

## Product Purpose
Make AI-assisted product delivery reliable, explainable, and continuously improvable.

## Primary User
Engineering and product teams coordinating multiple AI-assisted workstreams.

## Core Job to Be Done
Move from a real user problem to a verified change while preserving intent, scope, evidence, and accountability.

## Product Promise
Every shipped behavior can explain why it exists, who approved it, how it was verified, and whether it improved the intended outcome.

## Not This
- We are not a generic project-management replacement.
- We do not optimize throughput at the expense of outcome quality.
- We do not allow unreviewed high-risk autonomy.

# Product Brief

## Problem
Repository delivery systems start too late at the PRD and cannot prove that proposed features originate from evidenced customer problems and measurable outcomes.

## Primary User
Product and engineering leaders using AI agents across complex repositories.

## Desired User Outcome
Approved work has an unbroken chain from outcome and opportunity through operational proof.

## Business Importance
This prevents feature-factory drift and makes governance useful before code is written.

## Constraints
- Time: five-minute orientation and low-friction artifact creation.
- Budget: no mandatory external service.
- Team: explicit owners and human review boundaries.
- Technical: Markdown and JSON with standard-library validation.
- Market: portable across agent platforms.

## Out of Scope
- Automatic customer research collection.
- Automatic publication to external communities.
- A hosted product-management database.

## Decision Needed
Adopt `.ai/` as the canonical upstream product layer while keeping delivery artifacts under `docs/`.

## Hypothesis
A guided pre-hypothesis funnel — capture signals and sources, explore markets and recurring workflows, drill into candidate segments (roles, consequences, alternatives, accessibility), compare unvalidated candidates, promote one — lets a real Proofstack dogfood session reach a falsifiable investigation with provenance attached and no synthetic inputs.

## Test Design
Run one real niche exploration as a dogfood session. The first pass may use a manual paper funnel (documents mirroring the five stages) while the product flow is unbuilt; once BET-002 ships its smallest valuable version, repeat the exploration inside Proofstack. Record: signals captured with sources, markets and workflows explored, candidate segments drilled, comparison notes, the promotion decision, and the provenance attached to the resulting investigation. The seeded synthetic demo hypothesis (accounting example in `seed.ts`) stays excluded and labeled throughout.

## Success Threshold
The session produces at least one promoted investigation whose hypothesis is falsifiable and links its sources, explored candidates, and comparison rationale; zero synthetic demo records are referenced as evidence; and A-004, A-005, and A-006 each receive a recorded observation.

## Failure Signal
The session cannot proceed without inventing a hypothesis up front, the promoted investigation carries no provenance, or synthetic seed data leaks into evidence.

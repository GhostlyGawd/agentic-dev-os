# Research Notes

## Observations
- PRDs are strong delivery contracts but weak discovery records.
- Stable paths matter; moving task files between status folders breaks references.
- A hidden `.ai` directory is useful for agent orientation if canonical boundaries are explicit.
- Proofstack dogfood (2026-07-13): the product manages and validates supplied hypotheses well but does not help discover which hypothesis deserves investigation; creating an investigation requires an already-written segment/workflow summary, and Market, Segment, Hypothesis, and Evidence records are manual entry.
- Proofstack dogfood (2026-07-13): the seeded worked example (an accounting niche in `seed.ts`) is a synthetic demo hypothesis with fictional evidence and founder-access claims; it must be labeled as synthetic at every reference and never cited as market validation (FINDING-001).
- Proofstack dogfood (2026-07-13): the product spec promises a source → observe → define path; the missing stages are capture signals/sources → explore markets and recurring workflows → drill candidate segments (roles, consequences, alternatives, accessibility) → compare unvalidated candidates → promote one with provenance (dogfood issue #5).

## Signals Worth Validating
- Time from product question to validated bet.
- Percentage of change requests rejected before implementation.
- Product-chain coverage across active PRDs.
- Share of Proofstack sessions that start from raw signals rather than a formed hypothesis (A-004).
- Whether promotion-time provenance keeps synthetic and unvalidated inputs out of validation claims (A-005).
- How often candidate comparison changes which hypothesis is promoted (A-006).

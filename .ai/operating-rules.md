# Product Operating Rules

- No PRD without a linked validated BET.
- No BET without a linked opportunity and outcome.
- No opportunity without evidence and a measurable outcome.
- No outcome without metric, baseline, target, horizon, and guardrails.
- No milestone without a bet, PRD, exit criteria, and review record.
- No ticket without a PRD requirement and spec criterion.
- No scope change without an approved change request.
- No milestone closes without a human review log.
- No release closes without an outcome observation plan.
- No feature enters execution directly from conversation.
- Generated requirement and CSV views are never hand-edited.

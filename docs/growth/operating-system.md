# Growth Operating System

## Audience and hypothesis

Serve AI-native engineering, developer-tool, platform, and governance-conscious teams experiencing spec drift, rework, unclear scope, or audit gaps. The hypothesis is that naming the pain, teaching a standalone method, showing a working artifact, and offering a five-minute demo will convert qualified attention into retained adoption.

## Messaging and channels

Lead with less rework, faster spec-to-code flow, safer agents, clearer reviews, and explainable ownership. Use GitHub, technical documentation, long-tail search, developer publishing, relevant community discussions, live walkthroughs, and founder narratives. Participate with useful answers before sharing links.

## Content, community, and sharing

Publish failure analyses, tutorials, comparisons, case studies, observability guides, and architecture examples. Operate office hours, Q&A, template sharing, demos, and public issue discussions. Product outputs—trace matrices, readiness checks, dashboards, specs, and loop summaries—must be copyable and shareable.

## SEO and conversion

Target specific developer problems and long-tail intent. Each page teaches the problem, method, example, and next useful action. The conversion path is: useful asset → README/docs → demo → first ticket → shadow-mode trial → adoption → retained use → case study or advocacy.

## Staged launch

Publish the problem story, philosophy, starter, walkthrough, evidence, feedback request, and improved release as separate useful moments. The twelve-week program in `strategy.md` controls cadence.

## Measurement

Record visitors, demos, trials, adoptions, retention, and advocates with `ados.py growth record`; generate conversion reports with `ados.py growth report`. Track stars, forks, downloads, subscribers, shares, mentions, community participation, and content conversions as channel context. Qualified adoption and retention outrank vanity traffic.

## Adapter boundary

The core never stores external credentials or publishes automatically. A future adapter must expose `publish_asset`, `read_channel_metrics`, and `list_feedback`, use least privilege, redact user data, and emit growth events. Destination selection and credentials require explicit user authorization.

## Weekly operating loop

Publish one durable asset, share one example, answer questions, improve one onboarding surface, capture one user insight, review the funnel, decide one experiment, and turn the learning into the next asset.
